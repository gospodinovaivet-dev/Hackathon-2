Where things go

Each part of the fridge has its own temperature

The bottom shelf is the coldest and the door is the warmest. Put each food where it lasts longest.

Top shelf4–5 °C
Leftovers, hummus, ready-to-eat food, drinks
Middle shelf3–4 °C
Milk, yogurt, eggs, cheese
Bottom shelf1–3 °C
Raw meat and fish, sealed, on a plate Lowest, so drips can't fall on other food
Crisper drawerhumid
Vegetables, salad leaves, fruit Keep apples apart from greens
Door5–7 °C
Sauces, jam, juice, butter Warms up every time you open it. No milk or eggs here.
ColdestWarmest

Move these

  • Don't overfill. Cold air needs space to move.
  • Older food to the front. You'll eat it first.
  • Let hot food cool before it goes in, but within 2 hours.

What one tap does

  1. Your phone sends “take photo” to the fridge.
  2. The fridge turns its inside light on for 2 seconds.
  3. Both cameras take a photo.
  4. The photos upload and the AI updates your food list.
Where the cameras sit Cam 1 is a wide-angle (160°) camera at the top of the door's inner panel. When the door is closed it faces every shelf and the front of the crisper. Cam 2 sits in the ceiling and points down at the door racks. Both are ESP32-CAM boards with a heated lens so they don't fog up.
